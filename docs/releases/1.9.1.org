#+AUTHOR: Andrey Pudov
#+DATE: <2026-10-02 Fri>
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

A fix for Macs where CoreEQ 1.9 stopped equalizing whenever anything played,
and said “Paused while recording” with nothing recording.

* Fixed

- *Siri listening was mistaken for a recording.* 1.9 pauses while a screen
  recording captures system audio, so the recording does not come out with an
  echo. It recognised a recording by how the capture looked — audio captured
  from no input device — and the service that listens for “Hey Siri”, and also
  serves dictation and Live Captions, looks the same. On Macs with *Listen for
  Siri* turned on, CoreEQ paused as soon as anything played.

  CoreEQ now recognises a recording by what is capturing: macOS's screen
  capture service, which records on behalf of every screen recorder and every
  app that shares a screen with sound. Siri, dictation, and apps using the
  microphone no longer pause it. Screen recordings with audio still do.

If you turned off *Pause while recording system audio* to work around this, it
is safe to turn it back on in Settings → General.
